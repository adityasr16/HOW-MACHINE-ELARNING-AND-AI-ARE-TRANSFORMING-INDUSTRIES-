## Full read (one paragraph, for TTS)

> Wood is losing. Here's why U.S. builders are making the switch. Every year,
> ten-point-eight billion dollars is lost to the framing labor shortage. And
> material costs? Up over forty-one percent since 2020. So builders are switching
> to light-gauge steel framing. Reason one — it frames faster; prefab panels go
> up in days, not weeks. Reason two — it needs fewer hands: less labor, faster
> training, fewer field calls. Reason three — it outlasts wood: non-combustible,
> termite-proof, built to last a hundred years. Reason four — prices stay steady;
> steel doesn't swing seventy percent in a year. Building your first LGSF project?
> We've detailed hundreds of them across twelve countries. Let's build yours.
> UBC BIM — Unique Building Concepts.
